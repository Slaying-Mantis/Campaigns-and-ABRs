Staff Reporter Istrid Pebble | 16th Marpenoth, 1495

**UNDERCLIFF VILLAGE:** A farm hand goes missing during the night of Gods' Day, as reported by the local meadery to the Lords' Alliance offices in Waterdeep. The missing person, Maeren Hatch, was employed as part of the staff of Snobeedle Orchard & Meadery. Staff said he was last seen attending the prayer pyres set in honor of Chauntea. The staff reported his disappearance to the estate, who then sent word out to the Lords' Alliance. Investigators are still conducting their inquiries into the disappearance; however, no credible leads have yet been established. The Lords' Alliance maintains confidence that their investigators will do a thorough job. They also refused any updates and denied any correlation to previous disappearances of a similar nature where no credible leads were ever uncovered. The list of without-a-trace disappearances may yet have another name if the whereabouts of Mr. Hatch are never discovered.
