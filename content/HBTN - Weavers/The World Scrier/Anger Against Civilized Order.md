Staff Reporter Haiden Kavera | 15th Nightal, 1495 DR

**GOLDENFIELDS:** The residents of villages and towns along the major highways of the Sword Coast have been raising their voice against the inaction of the Lords' Alliance in preventing the giant raids across the Savage Frontier. Many farming co-ops have already drafted petitions seeking permissions to hire their own security to safeguard against possible raids. 

Brandon, a farmer from Goldenfields, says, "We can't wait for the Lords' Alliance to wake from its stupor to safeguard ourselves. They are all pawns of the Open Lord of Waterdeep. The Open Lord is secretly controlled by the mages of Silverymoon, and they only care about their artefacts and spells. We must take matters into our hands. Thanks to the support of the members of the Purple Order of Freedom, we have been able to draft petitions to break away from the Lords' Alliance. The order of the civilized does not care for the rural folk."

This is just the latest in the long due frustrations against the Alliance for not being able to make safe the rural towns and villages of the Sword Coast.
