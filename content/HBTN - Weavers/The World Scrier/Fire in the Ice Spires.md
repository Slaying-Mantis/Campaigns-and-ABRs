Special Correspondent Trevor Manns | 1st Uktar, 1495 DR

**SILVER MARCHES:** A wave of refugees arrived at the gates of Citadel Adbar at the turn of the season, after being driven from their homes by raiding yakfolk and fire giants. Many of the refugees also claimed to have seen a great pillar of smoke rising from the heart of the Ice Spires, a peculiar phenomenon that requires further investigation. King Harnoth of Citadel Adbar agreed to foster the refugees for a short while after a special request was made to the Lords' Alliance and the Emerald Enclave. 

The Lords' Alliance has released an official statement asking travelers to move in numbers and hire caravan guards whenever possible. Similar reports of raids by giants have been increasing in number across the Savage Frontier, especially on the less busy highways. Unnecessary travel should be avoided as recommended by the Alliance.
