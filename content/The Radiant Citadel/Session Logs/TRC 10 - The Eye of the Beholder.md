# Log
---
- We made our way into the broken portion of the Draught Elder.
- The terrain here was sundered apart in a void where even gravity was weakened. Most of us had to carefully jump across the various floating platforms traverse this space. As we were making our way across, we were confronted by another minion of the Draught Elder - Ozk, the beholder!

- After [[Bhumi]] and [[Ojha]] tore into the beholder, while [[Aruna]] saved a petrified [[Hayathe]], we finally made our way to the other side. The long winding body of the elder finally ended at a portion that seemed to open out into a haunting void.
- Right before the opening, where the winding body evolved into a terrifying head with mandibles, a strange glow filled the space. As we stepped close to investigate, a shape grew out of the glow, snaking its way into its full form. The Aspect of the Elder had finally shown itself.
- We have not yet seen any signs of the shard stolen from the Ghost Orchid Tepui. 

# Summary
---
- While traversing the broken terrain floating in the void, we encountered a beholder that was lying in wait. 
- We overcame the challenge, crossed the broken terrain and then arrived the "head" of this winding body where possibly the champion/aspect of the Elder lies in wait. 
- We are yet to locate the shard from the Ghost Orchid Tepui.
