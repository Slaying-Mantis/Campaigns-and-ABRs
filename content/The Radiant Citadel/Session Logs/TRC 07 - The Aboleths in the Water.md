# Log
---
- [[Yarana]] had managed to snap out of the trance temporarily, and quickly told us that there are two Aboleths in the water. 

> [!Initiative]
> Ojha
> Bhumi
> Yarana
> Moksha
> Aruna

- [[Ojha]] hanging close to [[Moksha]] in case of any support is needed. 
- As she says this, a deep guttural voice says - "If you would like to go in peace, do not touch the pale flowers."
	- [[Bhumi]] and [[Aruna]] notice two seed pods on the tree - 1 black and 1 white
- [[Moksha]] becomes charmed by one of the Aboleths. However, [[Aruna]], [[Ojha]] and [[Bhumi]] are together able to dispatch the two aboleths pretty fast. 
- Yarana tells us that the aboleths had kept her charmed. [[Chimagua]] is dying.
> "That (pointing at the crystal) is sleeping stone. The source of Chimgua's power. This was what he had used to influence the dreams of the people of Atague. But, the power of the Draught Elder has corrupted this crystal. This caused Chimagua to go into a forced slumber and then cause the planar instability. I have touched it and seen the visions of the Draught Elder. It is an expanse of broken worlds, in which sits a gargantuan alien form. Close to the shape of a centipede, yet so far. This form is shattered, convulsion. If you go close to it, the hollow eyes of the Elder fix on you. Yet, from far, it looks like a raging storm with changing colors. Recently, creatures from the far realm slipped into Atagua. They managed to break the sleeping stone and took a piece away. If that shard can be returned, there is hope that Chimagua can be restored."
> "A relic of Atagua's past may aid us in reaching the Draught Elder. The [[Hammock of Worlds]] may help unlock the path to the Draught Elder."

- [[Bhumi]] asks her what she knows about the white seed pod. 
> "The Orchid plants themselves are manifestations of the Chimagua's influence. Both seed pods are magical. The black seed pod holds 3 seeds. The white seed pod holds 1 seed. If you eat the black seed pod, you are under the effect of Feign Dead. If you powder and scatter the white seed pod on a dead, it is resurrected."

- The Dawn Mother holds the [[Hammock of Worlds]]. She frequents a oasis in Atagua called the [[Basket]]. She is aloof, but she of the land. She may be swayed by our desire to aid the land. 
	- The [[Green Doctors]] might be able to help us use the Hammock. 
- Yarana asks us to take [[Nene]] with us on this next leg of the adventure.
- After formulating a plan forward, [[Aruna]] opens a path through a tree and takes us as close as possible to the Grass Roads that we have already traversed. 

# Summary
---
- The aboleths from under the water attacked us. We were able to emerge victorious after a couple of rounds. 
- [[Bhumi]] then healed Yarana. She then told us all the events that had transpired over the last couple of days. She told asked us if we would be able to retrieve the stolen shard. 
- After some planning and strategizing, we teleported to the Grass Roads.
