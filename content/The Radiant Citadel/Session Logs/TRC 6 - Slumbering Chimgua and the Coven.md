# Log
---
**Inside the Cave**
- There is a pillar on the other side of the brackish pool; carved into is the relief of two serpents coiling around each other with glowing green eyes. 
- We found a 100 ft. long snake skin at the bottom of the pool. As soon as we pulled the skin out, the pool cleared up and the green vapors cleared.
- Hayathe discerned that the snake skin is probably diseased. 
- There is another small cavern area, where there are old bedrolls and an old altar with an old censer. 
- As we are discussing various plausible theories behind the diseased snake skin, [[Aruna]] hears that dreaded whistling sound - whistlers attack!

> [!initiative]
> - Whistler 1 (dark)
> - Moksha
> - Whistler 2 (light)
> - Hayathe
> - Bhumi
> - Aruna

- After the fight, we take a short rest to recover and get ready for what comes next. 
- During the time, [[Aruna]] and [[Bhumi]] studied the herbs in the censer of this room. 
	- The herbs seem to some sort of psychedelic herb. During rest, we decided to light some of these herbs. 
	- [[Moksha]] passed out from it and got poisoned. [[Hayathe]] cured the poison with a Lesser Restoration. 

> [!Note]
> 
> Short Rest approved!

- [[Aruna]] also discovered a secret door behind the altar that had the censer. It led a large area - [[Cave Area 02.png]]
	- There is a large tree in the center. There are orchid vines growing on the tree. 
	- Three elves in clerical garb appear out of thin air in front of us. 
	- They desperately ask for our help and say that some creature is trying to kill. [[Moksha]] sense deceit in their voice and [[Bhumi]] see through this divine sense  to realize that they are fiends. 

> [!Initiative]
> - Bhumi
> - Hag 1, 2
> - Yarana
> - Moksha, Hayathe
> - Aruna
> - Hag 3

- [[Moksha]] goes toward the south eastern platform. There is a 10 ft. high chipped green crystal. In the eastern platfrom, we see [[Yarana]] with a anaconda coiled around here. 
- [[Bhumi]] skewers one of the hags and breaks the coven. Soon after, the other hags flee.
- As [[Moksha]] gets closers to Yarana, she attacks with a her sword; it seems as though she under the control of something else. 
- She manages to break out of that trance and says:
> "Watch out, there are two aboleths in the water still."

# Summary
---
- We investigated the starting area of the cave. There was a pillar with the relief carving of two interwined serpents. In front of the pillar, inside a brackish pool was a diseased discarded skin of a 100 ft. long serpent. Once removed, the pool's water cleared up. 
- We investigated a small cavern space adjacent to the larger cavern. While in there, we were attacked by whistlers. 
- [[Aruna]] found a secret door that led to a much larger cavern where a tree stood in the middle of a small island connected by wooden bridges. The tree had orchid vines growing all over it. 
- Three hags tried to deceive us, but didn't succeed. As the fight went, [[Bhumi]], [[Hayathe]], and [[Aruna]] made quick work of one of the hags, which prompted the other two to flee before they were skewered. 
- In the eastern most ledge, [[Moksha]] found [[Yarana]] in some sort of trance with Chimagua coiled around her, slumbering in a magical sleep.
