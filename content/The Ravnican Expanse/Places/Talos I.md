**Talos I** is a [[Qurium|qurium]] mining station in the [[Gauss Belt]]. It is one of the many mobile mining stations constructed by the [[Izzet League]]. It houses ~200 miners, with a total output of 2 tons every month. Commissioned in 10,024 ZC by the [[Izzet League]], it was handed over to the belter factions at the beginning of the Miner Welfare and Subsidized Energies Mandate. It is currently owned by the [[Gauss Miners' Union]]. 

# Description
---
**Talos I** is now several decades into its deployment in the [[Gauss Belt]] and so has lost most of its sheen; currently held together by patchwork repairs and  ugly welds. It's asteroid shields are a canvas of dents and tears from its decades long service. The main mining hub houses a massive hook and drill used to pull in mineral rich asteroids for mining. A large rotating dish helps the hub locate mineral rich rocks and detect hazardous rock winds. Massive chains originating from the Mine itself anchor the Affixes to main rock. When a rock wind is detected, the Affixes and corollaries turtle close to the Mine, allowing the various asteroid barriers to full enclose the station and protect the internal structures. 

## Affixes
---
**Talos I** has several Affixes attached to the main rock. These affixes of various sizes and shapes form the Miner Dormitories, Mess Hall, Miner Union Exchange, Boros Outpost, and minor residences.
