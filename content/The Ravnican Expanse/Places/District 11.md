**District 11** is a massive space port sitting just beyond the Gauss Belt, which forms the main seat of power for the Gauss Miners' Union. The Union itself is formed by seven elected heads from the various Belter Factions, along with one representative each from the [[Azorius Senate]] and [[Orzhov Syndicate]]. 
# Description
---
It is a space port whose myriad layers of ship docks and piers create the semblance of a floating city. Each ship dock is a rented space and can be occupied and reserved as long as one can pay. Ships of various sizes and makes fill the numerous docks of the port, with the biggest usually belonging to the strongest factions or one of the many guilds conducting business in the port. 

Many of the Guilds have embassies present here, except for the [[Azorius Senate]], as their authority is not recognized for internal Belter matters. The many Belter factions maintain their HQs within this port and therefore set the laws of the port, via the Miners' Union. The [[Orzhov Syndicate]] and the [[Izzet League]] are allowed to operate from the port itself, for reasons know only by the elected members of the Union.
