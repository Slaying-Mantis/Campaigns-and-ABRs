**The Proletariat** is a shrike ship, covered in glitter, used by the community on [[Faith's End]], formerly owned by [[Dahlia]], the ex-[[Boros Legion]] commander. 

# Ship Stats
---
**Armor Class**: 15 (wood)

**Hit Points**: 200 ([[The Proletariat#^c94bad|250]]) 

**Damage Threshold:** 15 

**Speed:** fly 70 ft.  


**Cargo**: 20 tons

**Crew:** 11 

**Keel/Beam**: 100 ft./ 20 ft.

## 3 Ballistae (3 Crew Each)
---
**Armor Class**: 15 

**Hit Points**: 50 

**Cost:** 50 GP (ballista), 5 GP (bolt)

It takes 1 action to load a ballista, 1 action to aim it, and 1 action to fire it.

***Bolt.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 120/480 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (3d10) piercing damage.

## Piercing Ram
---
The ship's spelljammer can make the following attack when the ship runs into another object or into a Gargantuan creature (see "[Crashing](https://5e.tools/book.html#AAG,2,crashing,0)").

***Piercing Ram.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, range 0 ft., one object or Gargantuan creature. *Hit:* 88 (16d10) piercing damange. The shrike ship takes half as much damage and comes to a dead stop. *Miss:* The attack deals no damage, the target moves into the nearest unoccupied space that isn't in the shrike ship's path, and the shrike ship can continue moving if it has any movement left. 

## Special Effects
---
- The ship is always 50 HP less that its max, until the entire is replaced or upgraded. ^c94bad
- When the ship drops below **150 HP**, the rudder starts ***drifting left***, until this damage is repaired. While the ship is drifting left, the spelljammer has to make a DC 15 Constitution check to turn right.
- When ship drops below **120 HP**, if begins to lose speed. First the reduction in speed is by ***10 ft***. If the ship drop below **70 HP**, the speed reduced a further ***20 ft***.
