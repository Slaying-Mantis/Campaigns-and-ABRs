# Ravnican Sol System
---
The Ravnican Expanses is a binary star system, with the two stars, Isa and Ursa, locked in orbit around each other. Ravnica was the last remaining naturally habitable planet after the Nova event of Ursa, when it transitioned from a main-sequence star to a white dwarf star. This event also scattered the plasma core of the star, depositing mineral rich debris in orbit around the two stars. This mineral would soon come to be called Qurium (star iron), soon after a fateful meteor made landfall in Ravnica. Significant amounts of these cooled mineral rich rocks fell into orbit around the secondary star Isa, forming the three asteroid belts - Ramanujan, Gauss, and Euler. 

[[Isa]] - Main-sequence secondary star
[[Ursa]] - White dwarf primary star.
